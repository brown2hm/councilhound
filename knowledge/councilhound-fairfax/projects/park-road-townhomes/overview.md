## Official record

The applicant is proposing to build 13 three story, 2 car garage townhomes on 1.18 acres.

- **Requests:** Rezone the 1.18 acre site from CR Commercial Retail zoning district to the RT Residential Townhouse zoning district. Special Exception to Section 4.5.6.B pertaining to Street trees and 10-foot landscape strip along the private street. Special Exception to Section 3.6.1 pertaining to required yard setbacks
