## Member commentary

- [Bates](https://councilhound.net/members/billy-bates) — At the March 11, 2025 work session, Bates expressed support for moving the Pickett Road Connector Trail to the west side and merging it with the sidewalk if feasible without losing funding. On April 8, 2025, he voted yes to pause expenditures on the trail until after the April 22, 2025 work session.
- [Hall](https://councilhound.net/members/stacy-hall) — At the March 11, 2025 work session, Hall requested more information on the Pickett Road Connector Trail. On April 8, 2025, she voted yes to pause expenditures on the trail until after the April 22, 2025 work session.
- [Hardy-Chandler](https://councilhound.net/members/stacey-hardy-chandler) — On April 8, 2025, Hardy-Chandler voted no on the motion to pause expenditures on the Pickett Road Connector Trail.
- [McQuillen](https://councilhound.net/members/rachel-mcquillen) — On April 8, 2025, McQuillen voted no on the motion to pause expenditures on the Pickett Road Connector Trail.
- [Amos](https://councilhound.net/members/anthony-amos) — On April 8, 2025, Amos abstained from the vote to pause expenditures on the Pickett Road Connector Trail.
