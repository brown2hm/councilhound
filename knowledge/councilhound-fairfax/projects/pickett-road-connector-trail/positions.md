## Open questions

- Whether any scope or cost changes to the trail project will trigger a SmartScale rescoring, and whether the project would retain its funding if rescored against the original 2018 applicant pool (raised 2025-07-08 City Council).
- Whether the public outreach meeting directed for Foxcroft Colony and Scout on the Circle residents in October took place as planned, and what its outcomes were (2025-07-22 City Council).

## Member commentary
