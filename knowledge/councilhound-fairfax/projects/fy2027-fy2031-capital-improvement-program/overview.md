The FY2027–FY2031 Capital Improvement Program (CIP) is a multi-year municipal capital planning document currently under review by the Planning Commission. The Commission has been conducting a series of work sessions on the proposed CIP since at least November 24, 2025, with staff presenting the report and discussions continuing across multiple meetings. Planning Division Chief Paul Nabti presented the staff report at the December 8, 2025 session. As of the January 12, 2026 work session, the Commission has not yet voted or issued recommendations; the process is advancing toward a public hearing and formal recommendation to City Council.
