The Old Town Parking Study is a city-commissioned project that analyzed parking utilization across Old Town, with field observations conducted by a consultant in the fall of 2024. The study's primary purpose was to evaluate how upcoming specific redevelopments in Old Town would impact parking in the area, though it yielded broad data on parking utilization across lots, retail centers, and office properties throughout the district. A presentation on the study was delivered to City Council during a work session on June 3, 2025, with no formal action taken. Staff noted to the Planning Commission on June 23, 2025 that the study's results would serve as a reference for future decisions, particularly related to the small area plan. The study was subsequently completed in 2025, with its key finding being that no additional parking structure is needed in Old Town and that future demand should instead be accommodated through shared parking. Study data — including peak utilization rates for retail centers (Main Street Marketplace and Courthouse Plaza, at roughly 2–2.5 spaces per 1,000 sq ft) and office properties around Old Town — was being actively referenced in parking standards discussions as of March 2026.
