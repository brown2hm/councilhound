## Official record

The potential applicant is proposing to convert an existing 7-story office building to mixed-use with 107 multifamily units on upper floors and ground floor non-residential uses and "live/work units" on approximately 3.25 acres. The subject site is zoned CR Commercial Retail and is in the Old Town Fairfax Transition Overlay District. The subject site is located in the Commercial Corridor Place Type, as designated in the Comprehensive Plan.

- **Requests:** Potential applications include an amendment to existing proffers, special exceptions, general development plan, and a major certificate of appropriateness for architecture and landscaping.
- **Address:** 10560 Main Street, Fairfax, VA 22030
- **Applicant:** Robert D. Brant Walsh, Colucci, Lubeley & Walsh, P.C.
