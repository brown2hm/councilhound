The Urban Forest Master Plan (UFMP) is the City of Fairfax's first 20-year roadmap for stewardship of its urban forest, developed with consultant Marstel-Day. Development began in May 2024; Phase 1 (information gathering) was completed and Phase 2 (plan development) was nearing completion as of December 2025. The December 2025 draft was unanimously recommended for approval by the Planning Commission (5-0) on February 9, 2026, and adopted by City Council via resolution R-26-07 (6-0) on February 10, 2026, following a public hearing. A substitute motion that would have required the Urban Forester to report back to Council by July 2026 on targets and timelines failed 2-4 before the main adoption vote. Since adoption, the plan has been cited as a driver of related policy work, including a tree conservation ordinance and landscaping standards being developed as part of broader zoning ordinance amendments. On July 14, 2026, Council received a work session update on the 1-year plan and 5-year outlook; no action was taken at that session.
