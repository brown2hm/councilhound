## Open questions

- What will the final design of the Wilcoxon Trail Extension be (shared-use path continuation vs. cycle track/two-way bike lane on Draper Drive)?
- Will VDOT approve the proposed alignment modification for the Wilcoxon Trail Extension within Smart Scale parameters?
- How will the city achieve broader multimodal connectivity that the George Snyder Trail was intended to provide, including connections toward Cardinal Road, Fairwoods Parkway Neighborway, and Fairfax Circle?
- What, if any, repayment obligations to VDOT resulted from the project cancellation (the estimated $3.7M repayment figure was discussed but no final resolution on financing was recorded)?

## Member commentary

- [Hall](https://councilhound.net/members/stacy-hall) — On January 28, 2025, Councilmember Hall inquired about the scheduling of the George Snyder Trail presentation and requested that the cost of canceling the project be included in that future presentation. Hall voted against reaffirming the full project in June 2025 (R-25-20) and abstained on the December 2025 supplemental appropriation introduction, ultimately voting no on the $4.6M appropriation on January 13, 2026.
- [Peterson](https://councilhound.net/members/thomas-peterson) — Councilmember Peterson noted in March 2025 that the Council was working toward a permanent resolution. Peterson sought a CFO repayment structure analysis for the potential $3.7M VDOT repayment at the May 2025 work session. Peterson was the sole vote in favor of outright cancellation in June 2025, voted no on the December 2025 supplemental appropriation introduction, and voted no on the $4.6M appropriation on January 13, 2026.
- [Bates](https://councilhound.net/members/billy-bates) — Councilmember Bates stated in March 2025 his support for canceling the Country Club Hills Connector Trail and moving the Pickett Road Connector Trail to the west side. Bates attended the VDOT meeting in late March 2025 alongside Councilmember Peterson. Bates was the sole vote in favor of the hybrid western-half-only option in June 2025, voted yes on the December 2025 supplemental appropriation introduction, and voted yes on the $4.6M appropriation on January 13, 2026. In May 2026, Bates thanked staff for their proactive approach on the Wilcoxon Trail Extension and expressed hope for a future connection to Chambers Road and the I-66 trail.
- [Amos](https://councilhound.net/members/anthony-amos) — At the May 2025 work session, Councilmember Amos indicated that the question of a CFO repayment structure analysis should wait until a cancellation decision is made. Amos voted yes on reaffirming the full project (R-25-20) in June 2025, yes on the December 2025 supplemental appropriation introduction, and yes on the $4.6M appropriation on January 13, 2026.
- [Hardy-Chandler](https://councilhound.net/members/stacey-hardy-chandler) — Councilmember Hardy-Chandler voted yes on reaffirming the full project (R-25-20) in June 2025, yes on the December 2025 supplemental appropriation introduction, and yes on the $4.6M appropriation on January 13, 2026. Hardy-Chandler made the motion to cancel the project on January 13, 2026 (per the transcript, the cancellation resolution motion was made by Hardy-Chandler and seconded by Peterson, passing unanimously). Hardy-Chandler was also noted as emphasizing the use of all boards and commissions, including Village in the City, in discussions about ADA accessibility and walkability in the context of the trail's cancellation.
- [McQuillen](https://councilhound.net/members/rachel-mcquillen) — Councilmember McQuillen voted yes on reaffirming the full project (R-25-20) in June 2025 but voted no on both the December 2025 supplemental appropriation introduction and the $4.6M appropriation on January 13, 2026.
