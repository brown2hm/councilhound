The George Snyder Trail was a planned shared-use path project (VDOT UPC 112816) in the City of Fairfax aimed at improving multimodal connectivity along Fairfax Boulevard. The project received $20.6 million in I-66 Outside the Beltway concessionaire funding. After years of planning, the project faced sustained public opposition. In April 2025, Council voted unanimously (6-0) not to advertise the project in its current configuration and directed staff to explore modifications. In June 2025, Council adopted Resolution R-25-20 (4-2) reaffirming the full project as designed, defeating both a hybrid western-half-only option and a full cancellation motion. By late 2025, construction bids came in $4.6 million over the remaining $16 million budget, requiring a supplemental appropriation from additional concessionaire funds. On January 13, 2026, that $4.6 million supplemental appropriation failed on a 3-3 tie vote, and Council subsequently voted 4-2 to cancel the George Snyder Trail Project in its entirety. Following cancellation, the associated Smart Scale-funded extension component (approximately $9.5 million in FY26 funding) is being reconceived as the Wilcoxon Trail Extension, with a revised alignment utilizing Draper Drive and Beach Drive rather than connecting to the now-cancelled trail's eastern terminus; as of May–June 2026, Council gave consensus to proceed with a feasibility study and public outreach on that revised concept.
