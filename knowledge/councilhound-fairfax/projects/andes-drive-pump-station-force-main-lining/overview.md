The Andes Drive Pump Station Force Main Lining project involves the rehabilitation of force main infrastructure at the Andes Drive pump station. On November 12, 2024, the City Council unanimously approved (6-0) a construction contract awarded to J. Fletcher Creamer & Son, Inc. for an estimated $834,892 as part of the consent agenda. After work commenced, additional needs were identified, including replacement of force main infrastructure inside the wet well and additional excavations and lining of the 8-inch force main. On November 18, 2025, the Council unanimously approved (6-0) a contract modification increasing the contract value by $349,904, bringing the total project cost to $1,184,796. The project is associated with the stormwater utility program and is currently in progress.
