10340 Democracy Lane is a site in the Old Town Fairfax Activity Center where a developer is exploring a potential multifamily residential redevelopment. The concept proposes replacing approximately 59,983 sq ft of existing office space with a 6-story multifamily residential building totaling approximately 434,000 sq ft on 3.22 acres — consistent with the small area plan's recommendation for new construction (residential, commercial, or mixed-use) with a six-story height limit. As of early 2026, the project remains in the pre-application phase; staff have held concept meetings with the applicant, but no formal application has been submitted. The Planning Commission received a pre-application briefing on February 9, 2026, followed by a City Council pre-application work session on February 24, 2026, presented by City Planner Clara Schweiger. The March 9, 2026 Planning Commission staff report confirmed both briefings had occurred and referenced the project among ongoing pre-application discussions.

## Official record

The potential applicant is proposing to redevelop the property with a six-story multifamily building featuring 260-280 residential units, with structured parking. The subject site is currently developed with an approximately 59,983-square-foot office building with surface parking. The subject site is currently zoned CR Commercial Retail in the Future Transition Overlay District. The subject site is located within the Activity Center Place Type as designated in the Comprehensive Plan.

- **Requests:** The applicant proposes to rezone approximately 3.22 acres from CR Commercial Retail to PD-R Planned Development Residential and Transition Overlay District. Potential applications include a rezoning, master development plan, special exceptions, and a major certificate of appropriateness.
