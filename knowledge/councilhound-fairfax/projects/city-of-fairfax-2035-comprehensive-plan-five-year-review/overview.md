The City of Fairfax 2035 Comprehensive Plan Five Year Review is a statutorily required periodic review (per Code of Virginia Section 15.2-2230) of the city's long-range land use and policy planning document. The process reached the Planning Commission stage by September 2024, with staff presenting the draft plan during a continued work session on September 9, 2024, at which no action was taken and a public hearing was tentatively scheduled for September 23, 2024. On September 23, 2024, the Planning Commission held that public hearing and unanimously adopted Resolution PC-24-03 (7-0), approving amendments to the plan as drafted on September 18, 2024 — subject to modifications incorporating City Council feedback, clarifying and confirming updated map graphics, and making related inline text edits — and formally recommending that City Council adopt the updated plan. The matter was then scheduled to advance to a City Council public hearing on October 8, 2024.
