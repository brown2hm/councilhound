The Fairfax HS Electrical Program Build-Out is a Schools Capital Improvement Program (CIP) project requiring a reappropriation of funds. The matter was first discussed at the City Council Work Session on November 4, 2025, as part of a broader agenda item that also addressed a reappropriation for the Providence ES vestibule project and a 0.5 FTE addition to the Schools Superintendent position; no formal vote was taken at that session. On November 18, 2025, the City Council approved the reappropriation resolution — covering both the Fairfax HS electrical program build-out and the Providence ES vestibule — as part of the consent agenda, with a unanimous vote. The project is now in an approved status.
