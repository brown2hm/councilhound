## Official record

The applicant, ABTB Mid-Atlantic, LLC, is proposing to develop a 2,090 square foot fast-food restaurant with a drive-through on approximately 0.66-acres. The subject site is currently a vacant pad site that is part of the Boulevard Marketplace Shopping Center.

- **Requests:** The applicant proposes an amendment to the Zoning and General Development Plan, a Special Use Permit for a fast-food restaurant with a drive-through in the CR Commercial Retail District in the Architectural Control Overlay (ACOD), and a Special Exception to provide an interior landscape island for every ten parking spaces. This land use application is subject to a Major Certificate of Appropriateness for architecture and landscaping.
