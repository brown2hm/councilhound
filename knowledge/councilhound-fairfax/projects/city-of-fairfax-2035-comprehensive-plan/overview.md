The City of Fairfax 2035 Comprehensive Plan is the city's long-range land use and policy framework document. A five-year review process, initiated in summer 2023 in accordance with Code of Virginia §15.2-2230, included community outreach, input from city boards and commissions, and a compilation and refinement phase through 2024. The Planning Commission held a work session and public hearing in September 2024, and on October 8, 2024, the City Council unanimously adopted Resolution R-24-30 approving the updated 2035 Comprehensive Plan dated September 27, 2024, completing the five-year review cycle. Separately, the Fairfax Circle Small Area Plan was approved by the Planning Commission on July 8, 2024 (Resolution PC-24-02, 5-0) and recommended to City Council, subject to final approval of associated Comprehensive Plan amendments. Staff has presented Implementation Guide updates related to the Plan and approved Small Area Plans at Planning Commission work sessions in July 2024 and a scheduled session in July 2026. Since adoption, a new potential review of the Plan relating to mixed-use development outside activity centers has been discussed by both the Planning Commission (March 2025) and City Council (September 2025), though no formal action has been taken on that initiative. A Comprehensive Plan Amendment was also requested in connection with the Highlands at Mantua project, which was the subject of a Planning Commission public hearing on July 27, 2026 (2026-07-27 Planning Commission Regular Meeting/Work Session, Agenda Item 6a); no minutes or actions report for that hearing is yet available.
