Chick-fil-A applied for a special use permit, special exceptions, and a certificate of appropriateness to modify its existing drive-through and building at 9509 Fairfax Boulevard (case nos. SU-24-00078/SE-24-00079). The proposed changes include a building addition, a dual drive-through lane, and widening of the pick-up window lane. At the September 9, 2025 City Council meeting, all three required motions — (1) the special use permit to modify the drive-through and building per City Code Section 6.7 (with three conditions), (2) special exceptions from transitional yard, building yard, and bulk plane requirements per City Code Section 110-6.16 (with two conditions), and (3) a certificate of appropriateness per City Code Section 110-6.5 (with three conditions) — were each approved unanimously by the full six-member Council. The approval was subsequently noted in the Planning Commission staff report at the October 13, 2025 regular meeting, confirming the matter is fully resolved at the municipal level.
