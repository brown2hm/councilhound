## Official record

The proposal would be the final phase of a PD-M District approved in 2020. The concept plan proposes one seven-story multifamily building and one upper-story mixed-use building connected by a seven-story parking garage, with up to 350 units, up to 7,000 sf of ground-floor commercial and approximately 450 parking spaces. Access is from Northfax Street and Chain Bridge Road.

- **Requests:** The potential applicant is requesting an amendment to an existing Master Development Plan with modifications, update to the existing commitments, and a Major Certificate of Appropriateness for architecture and landscaping. This proposed application would be subject to the City's Affordable Dwelling Unit (ADU) ordinance.
