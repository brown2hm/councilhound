The Stacy Sherwood Community Center project involves the expansion and integration of the existing Stacy C. Sherwood Community Center at 3740 Blenheim Boulevard into the larger Willard-Sherwood Health and Community Center development. The new health and community center, a joint project between the City of Fairfax and Fairfax County, will provide a broad range of health, social, and community services, with a bridge connection linking it to the existing Sherwood Center. On October 14, 2025, the City Council approved the necessary land-use entitlements — a Special Use Permit, two special exceptions (side yard modification and parking reduction), and a Certificate of Appropriateness — each by a 4-3 or 4-2 margin, with a building addition to the Stacy C. Sherwood Community Center approved as an optional component under each motion. On April 28, 2026, the Council unanimously adopted Resolution R-26-13 authorizing the project to proceed to construction, but explicitly excluding the optional performing arts space renovation and addition at the existing Sherwood Community Center, which will not proceed at this time. Amendments to the Joint Development Agreement with Fairfax County were also adopted as part of that resolution.
