## Open questions

- The optional performing arts space renovation and addition at the existing Stacy C. Sherwood Community Center was excluded from the approved construction resolution (R-26-13) and its future disposition — whether it will be reconsidered, deferred indefinitely, or abandoned — has not been determined.

## Member commentary

- [Hardy-Chandler](https://councilhound.net/members/stacey-hardy-chandler) — On October 14, 2025, Hardy-Chandler made the motions for both the special exceptions and the SUP, each including the condition that the Sherwood building addition be approved as an optional component. On April 28, 2026, Hardy-Chandler moved adoption of R-26-13 to proceed to construction, explicitly noting the exclusion of the optional performing arts space renovation, and expressed enthusiasm that the project was coming to fruition after having been involved since before taking office.
- [Amos](https://councilhound.net/members/anthony-amos) — Seconded Hardy-Chandler's motion on the special exceptions at the October 14, 2025 meeting.
- [Hall](https://councilhound.net/members/stacy-hall) — Voted against all three land-use motions on October 14, 2025, but voted yes on R-26-13 to proceed to construction on April 28, 2026.
- [McQuillen](https://councilhound.net/members/rachel-mcquillen) — Voted against the SUP and special exceptions on October 14, 2025, but voted yes on the Certificate of Appropriateness and yes on R-26-13 on April 28, 2026.
- [Peterson](https://councilhound.net/members/thomas-peterson) — Voted against all three land-use motions on October 14, 2025, but voted yes on R-26-13 to proceed to construction on April 28, 2026.
