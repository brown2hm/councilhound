## Open questions

- Whether the City Council will revise the Comprehensive Plan policy on residential development in commercial corridors (the policy question triggered by the Gatewood Plaza proposal, brought to Council as of September 9, 2025 following a Planning Commission majority vote).
- Whether and when formal action (Planning Commission recommendation and/or City Council decision) will be taken on the Gatewood Plaza Zoning Map Amendment, Comprehensive Plan Amendment, Special Use Permit, and nine special exceptions.
- Which specific City Council members informally requested the policy re-evaluation during the February 4, 2025 work session (noted as unresolved in the September 9, 2025 record).

## Member commentary

- **Clara Schweiger (staff presenter, CDMP)** — Schweiger served as the staff presenter at both the January 27, 2025 Planning Commission work session and the February 4, 2025 City Council work session, walking both bodies through the post-submission phase of the Gatewood Plaza redevelopment proposal, including the zoning and comprehensive plan requests and the nine special exceptions sought by the applicant.
- **Planning Division Chief Nabti** — Nabti briefed the Planning Commission on February 10, 2025 regarding recent City Council meetings, including the Gatewood Plaza City Council work session. He was also referenced as the staff resource at the September 9, 2025 City Council meeting regarding the origin of the policy re-evaluation request stemming from the Gatewood Plaza work session, though specific details of which council members made the informal request were not confirmed on the record at that time.
