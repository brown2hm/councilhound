Gatewood Plaza is a proposed redevelopment of an existing office building (and adjacent parking structure) at 10201 Fairfax Boulevard, located at a signalized intersection directly across from Boulevard Marketplace. The applicant is seeking a Zoning Map Amendment (from CR Commercial Retail and RM Residential Medium to CR Commercial Retail), a Comprehensive Plan Amendment (changing the future land use designation from Commercial Corridor to Multifamily Neighborhood), a Special Use Permit, and nine special exceptions covering density, bulk, building height, bulk plane, required yards, transitional yards, parking, and resource protection area. Post-application work sessions were held at the Planning Commission on January 27, 2025, and at the City Council on February 4, 2025; both were informational only with no formal action taken. A key procedural obstacle surfaced: under the current Comprehensive Plan, mixed-use development including residential uses is only permitted in designated activity centers or specific sites identified through the plan process, and the Gatewood Plaza site (located in a Commercial Corridor) does not qualify, meaning there was no mechanism to approve the proposal even with Planning Commission and City Council support. This prompted broader policy discussions — at both the Planning Commission and City Council levels — about whether the city should reevaluate its policy on residential development in commercial corridors, with the Planning Commission taking a majority vote recommending that question be brought forward to City Council, which was scheduled to take up that discussion as of September 2025.
