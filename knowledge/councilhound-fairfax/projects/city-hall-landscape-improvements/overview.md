The City Hall Landscape Improvements Project is a municipal construction project to improve landscaping at City Hall. On November 12, 2024, the City Council unanimously approved (6-0) the award of a construction contract to Galicia Construction Corporation for an estimated amount of $587,752 as part of the consent agenda. The project drew public opposition at the January 14, 2025 Council meeting, when resident Val Morgan spoke against it during the general public comment period.
