The CUE Transit Development Plan (TDP) is a planning project for the City's CUE (City University Express) transit system. The City Council first discussed the TDP during a work session on March 4, 2025, with no formal action taken at that time. A follow-up staff update was presented during another work session on October 7, 2025, again without a vote or formal action. The project appears to be in an ongoing planning and review phase, with Council being kept informed through periodic work session updates.
