The Noman Cole capital costs project involves financing capital improvements at the Noman Cole facility as part of the city's broader sewer system capital program. To fund these costs, the city pursued the issuance of sewer system revenue bonds of up to $11,000,000. On April 14, 2026, the City Council unanimously introduced the authorizing ordinance as part of the consent agenda. On May 12, 2026, following a public hearing that drew no public testimony, the Council unanimously approved Ordinance 2026-10 and Bond Resolution R-26-27, formally authorizing the issuance of up to $11,000,000 in sewer system revenue bonds to finance sewer system capital costs, including those related to Noman Cole. The bond authorization is now fully approved.
