The Chain Bridge Road/Eaton Place Intersection Improvements project involves proposed physical changes to the intersection of Chain Bridge Road and Eaton Place within the city. The project was first discussed by City Council during a Special Meeting/Work Session on February 18, 2025 (agenda item 4d), at which no formal action was taken. By March 25, 2025, the project had advanced to the intermediate design stage, at which point a public hearing was held (agenda item 7a) as required prior to right-of-way acquisition. Transportation Director Wendy Sanford presented the staff report at the March 25 hearing. Residents spoke both in support and in opposition to the project design; no Council action was required or taken at that hearing. The project is currently in progress, with right-of-way acquisition as the next anticipated phase.
