## Open questions

- City Council work session on the Courthouse Plaza application is scheduled for July 28, 2026 — no action has been taken yet by Council on the revised application.
- Staff has provided comments to the applicant following the first resubmission; further resubmissions or responses from the applicant are anticipated before a formal hearing.
- Phase 2 of the redevelopment (new street network, grocery anchor reorientation, ~315 additional units) is a longer-term element targeted after 2035; its specific plan and approvals have not yet been presented.
- The Safeway lease terms are stated to expire before Phase 2, allowing flexibility in the grocery anchor arrangement — but no formal lease or anchor commitment has been finalized.
- The Board of Architectural Review has provided design feedback (including on red brick placement) but its review process for the revised proposal is ongoing.
- The Planning Commission's role in reviewing private streets associated with the project remains an unresolved procedural question, flagged during the January 2026 bylaws discussion.

## Member commentary

- **Chair Feather (Planning Commission)** — On September 23, 2024, Chair Feather shared concerns with the mayor and council about the minimal commercial space proposed on the ground floor of the original proposal. He subsequently attended and provided comments at multiple Board of Architectural Review meetings on the Courthouse Plaza project, as reported at the March 24, 2025 and May 12, 2025 Planning Commission meetings, and again on June 8, 2026 (noting the BAR had met the prior week and received significant feedback).
- [Commissioner Cunningham](https://councilhound.net/members/paul-cunningham) — On January 12, 2026, Commissioner Cunningham cited Courthouse Plaza as an example of a major redevelopment involving private streets from which the Planning Commission had been excluded from review, using it to illustrate concerns about the commission's role in such projects during the bylaws discussion.
