## Open questions

- The Planning Commission held a public hearing on October 26, 2026; its formal recommendation to City Council has not yet been recorded. A formal City Council hearing date has not yet been set.
- Staff has provided comments to the applicant following resubmissions; further resubmissions or responses from the applicant may be anticipated before a formal hearing.
- Phase 2 of the redevelopment (new street network, grocery anchor reorientation, ~315 additional units) is a longer-term element targeted after 2035; its specific plan and approvals have not yet been presented. Phase 2 would require a future amendment to the master development plan (2026-07-28 City Council).
- The Safeway lease terms are stated to expire before Phase 2, allowing flexibility in the grocery anchor arrangement — but no formal lease or anchor commitment has been finalized.
- The Board of Architectural Review has provided design feedback (including on red brick placement) but its review process for the revised proposal is ongoing.
- The Planning Commission's role in reviewing private streets associated with the project remains an unresolved procedural question, flagged during the January 2026 bylaws discussion.
- The applicant is requesting a special exception to allow some sidewalks to be narrower than 10 feet, and a special exception to reduce the minimum parking ratio from 1.5 to 1.25 parking spaces per residential unit (2026-07-28 City Council) — neither has been acted on yet.
- A public commenter at the October 26, 2026 Planning Commission hearing called for the ADU requirement to be raised from 6% to 10%; whether the applicant or Commission will respond to this request is unresolved.

## Member commentary

- **Chair Feather (Planning Commission)** — On September 23, 2024, Chair Feather shared concerns with the mayor and council about the minimal commercial space proposed on the ground floor of the original proposal. He subsequently attended and provided comments at multiple Board of Architectural Review meetings on the Courthouse Plaza project, as reported at the March 24, 2025 and May 12, 2025 Planning Commission meetings, and again on June 8, 2026 (noting the BAR had met the prior week and received significant feedback).
- [Commissioner Cunningham](https://councilhound.net/members/paul-cunningham) — On January 12, 2026, Commissioner Cunningham cited Courthouse Plaza as an example of a major redevelopment involving private streets from which the Planning Commission had been excluded from review, using it to illustrate concerns about the commission's role in such projects during the bylaws discussion. At the October 26, 2026 Planning Commission public hearing, Commissioner Cunningham expressed support for the project, noting that the applicant had reduced the number of special exceptions requested (to one) and stating that this made the proposal easier to evaluate and that it would provide more predictability for future proposals in small areas.

## Public comment

- **Old Fairfax Muse Homeowners Association president** — At the July 28, 2026 City Council work session, expressed support for several elements of the revised proposal, including additional retail, mixed-use housing, and the inclusion of a park. Raised concerns about the proposed architecture, describing it as modern, stark, and a jarring contrast to Old Town, and called on Combined Properties to design the site as a locus of downtown activity, suggesting a public square surrounded by retail below and apartments above.
- **Jim Gillespie (3604 Colony Road)** — At the October 26, 2026 Planning Commission public hearing, spoke in support of the project, citing the community's need for housing. Suggested raising the ADU requirement from 6% to 10%, which he estimated would add approximately 11–12 affordable units in each phase, and noted that employees of nearby retail businesses, a nursing home, library, fire station, police station, two schools, and the soon-to-open senior center and child care center at Willard Sherwood Center would benefit from additional affordable units on site.
