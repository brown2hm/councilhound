The Citywide Level of Service Study is an ongoing analytical project in which the City of Fairfax has contracted consulting firm TischlerBise — described as having nationwide experience in economic and fiscal analysis — to develop a dynamic capital impact model. The model is intended to help the city estimate capital needs resulting from new development and redevelopment, particularly at the time of rezonings. A key driver is the lack of existing policy guidance on cash proffers: while Virginia jurisdictions commonly accept cash proffers as part of major rezoning projects, and Fairfax City has done so in the past, no framework currently exists for determining what proffers may be needed or how to calculate them. City Planner Eric Forman has been the primary staff presenter throughout the project. On 2026-02-24, Forman and TischlerBise consultants Julie Herlands and Jackson Barlow presented the project's goals, progress, and Land Use Assumptions to the City Council in a work session; no Council action was taken. On 2026-04-13, Forman provided a further update to the Planning Commission in a work session; discussion was held but no formal action was taken. On 2026-09-01, the City Council received a discussion and report on the findings of the Citywide Level of Service Study during a reconvened work session; no formal vote was recorded.
