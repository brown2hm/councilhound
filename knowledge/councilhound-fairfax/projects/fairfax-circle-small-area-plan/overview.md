The Fairfax Circle Small Area Plan is a land-use and transportation planning document for the Fairfax Circle area of the City of Fairfax, developed beginning in Spring 2023. The Planning Commission approved the plan via Resolution PC-24-02 on July 8, 2024 (on a 5-0 vote), recommending City Council adopt the June 24, 2024 draft subject to Comprehensive Plan amendment approval and specified modifications, including property access preservation, a transportation safety study, analysis of widening walkways/shared-use paths, and three textual edits. City Council held a public hearing on July 23, 2024 — with no public testimony — and adopted the plan (in an updated July 11, 2024 draft) on a 4-2 vote, with Councilmembers Doyle Feingold and Greenfield opposed. Following adoption, the plan has informed multiple downstream city efforts, including a Fairfax Circle intersection evaluation included in the city's two-year transportation program, stream restoration prioritization along Daniel's Run/Accutink Creek, Blenheim Boulevard multimodal improvement extensions, height and frontage recommendations in zoning documents, and flood/nature-based solutions studies. As of June 2026, a potential review and update of the plan (alongside the Old Town, Northfax, and Kamp Washington small area plans) was scheduled for Planning Commission work session discussion.
