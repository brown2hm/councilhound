## Open questions

- What are the specific project priorities and recommendations that will emerge from the Safety Action Plan, Pedestrian Master Plan, and Old Town Circulation Study?
- How will the City track progress on pedestrian safety targets over time (e.g., the pedestrian level of stress analysis referenced in the June 23, 2026 Steering Committee discussion)?
- What mechanism will be established to measure improvement at a city-wide level rather than site by site?
- Is the project on track for its mid-2026 completion target, given the June 2026 Steering Committee discussion still in progress?

## Member commentary

- **Chloe Ritter (Staff)** — As Multimodal Transportation Planner, Ritter presented the Move Fairfax City kickoff update to Council and the Planning Commission on July 1, 2025, explaining that the project's purpose is to take existing long-term plans and identify specific priorities for safety and pedestrian improvements across the city.
