Move Fairfax City is an umbrella transportation planning project for the City of Fairfax encompassing three components: a citywide Safety Action Plan, a citywide Pedestrian Master Plan, and an Old Town Circulation Study. The project's purpose is to translate existing long-term plans into specific, prioritized implementation steps for safety and pedestrian improvements. The project formally launched with a joint City Council and Planning Commission work session on July 1, 2025, at which staff Multimodal Transportation Planner Chloe Ritter provided the kickoff presentation. A Move Fairfax City Steering Committee was established, with resident representatives Jason Desai and Emily Rabbit appointed by unanimous Council vote on June 24, 2025, and Planning Commissioner Rice named as a committee member; the committee's first meeting was scheduled for August 2025. As of late July 2025, the Safety Action Plan and Old Town Circulation Study were reported as underway, with the overall project expected to be complete by mid-2026. One related project, the George Snyder Trail, was cancelled by a 4-2 Council vote on January 13, 2026, following a failed vote to award its construction contract; Northern Virginia Families for Safe Streets, which held a seat on the Move Fairfax City Advisory Committee, publicly supported the trail at that meeting.
