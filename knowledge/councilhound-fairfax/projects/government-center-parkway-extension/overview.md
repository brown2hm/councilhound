The Government Center Parkway Extension is a road construction project that has been included in the city's comprehensive plan for an extended period. On January 28, 2025, the City Council unanimously approved a construction contract in the estimated amount of $3,131,100.00 to Ardent Company, LLC as part of the consent agenda. Construction kicked off in early April 2025, and the project is anticipated to be completed by the end of 2026.

## Official record

Construction of the missing link of Government Center Parkway, including sidewalks, bike lanes, pedestrian crosswalks, lighting, and landscaping, connecting Stevenson Street in Fairfax County to Jermantown Road in the City of Fairfax.
