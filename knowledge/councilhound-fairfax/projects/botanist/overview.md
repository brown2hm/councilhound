## Official record

The applicant proposes to replace existing surface parking at WillowWood Office Plaza with a seven story building that consist of up to 260 apartments, approximately 5,000 square feet of ground floor retail, and a parking garage on approximately 2.97 acres. The subject property is located in the Northfax Small Area Plan.

- **Requests:** The applicant has requested a Zoning Map Amendment (Rezoning) from CR Commercial Retail to CU Commercial Urban in the Architectural Control Overlay District (ACOD), a Special Use Permit for upper story residential/mixed-use building, and Special Exceptions to density, height, 75% ground floor nonresidential use in a mixed use building, setbacks, sidewalks, tree canopy requirement and vehicular cross-access. This land use application is subject to the City's Affordable Dwelling Unit (ADU) ordinance and requires a Major Certificate of Appropriateness for architecture and landscaping.
