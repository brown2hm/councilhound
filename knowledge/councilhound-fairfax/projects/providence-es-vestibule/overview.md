The Providence ES Vestibule is a Schools Capital Improvement Program (CIP) project requiring a reappropriation of funds. The project was first discussed at a City Council Work Session on November 4, 2025, as part of a broader agenda item covering Schools CIP reappropriations and a 0.5 FTE addition to the Schools Superintendent position; no formal vote was taken at that session. On November 18, 2025, the City Council formally approved a reappropriation resolution covering the Providence ES vestibule project (alongside a related electrical program build-out at Fairfax HS and the 0.5 FTE Superintendent addition) as part of the consent agenda, with a unanimous vote. The project is now in an approved status.
