The Boulevard Marketplace Shopping Center GDP Amendment concerns a request by ABTB Mid-Atlantic, LLC to amend the General Development Plan for the shopping center at 10120 and 10140–10160 Fairfax Boulevard (Tax Map Parcels 47-4-23-000-A and 47-4-23-000-D) in order to construct a 2,090-square-foot Taco Bell restaurant with a drive-through and walk-up window. The project also required a Special Use Permit for the drive-through, a Special Exception for reduced interior parking landscaping islands, and a Major Certificate of Appropriateness for architecture and landscaping. On September 9, 2024, the Planning Commission unanimously recommended City Council approval of the GDP amendment (GDP dated August 19, 2024), while deferring action on the remaining three approvals. On December 10, 2024, the City Council approved all four items — adopting Ordinance No. 2024-14 for the GDP amendment, approving the Special Use Permit and Certificate of Appropriateness, and adopting Resolution R-24-39 for the Special Exception — each on a 4-2 vote, with Council Members Lim and Stehle opposed.
