The Northfax East-West Roadway is a City of Fairfax capital project to construct a new east-west roadway connecting Chain Bridge Road/University Drive Extension to Cambridge Road in the Northfax area. The project carries an estimated total cost of approximately $21 million, with roughly half funded by NVTA 70% funds and the other half by VDOT Revenue Sharing funds. The City Council first endorsed submission of a Revenue Sharing Funding application for the project on May 13, 2025, as part of Resolution R-25-09 alongside two other projects (1st/2nd Street Sidewalks and Dwight Avenue Improvements). On September 9, 2025, the Council unanimously adopted an updated resolution (R-25-27) reauthorizing submission of the same three projects to VDOT for Revenue Sharing Funding, with the combined application total updated to up to $10 million. Both votes were unanimous (6-0). The project remains in progress pending VDOT funding decisions.

## Official record

A new east-west roadway connecting University Drive at Point 50 to Chain Bridge Road will improve circulation for motorists, pedestrians, and cyclists.
