The Dwight Avenue Improvements project involves intersection realignment at Sager/Roberts/Dwight and installation of new sidewalk, curb, and gutter on the north side of Dwight Avenue between Roberts Road and Orchard Drive. The project was identified as early as December 2024 as a new capital project tied to the city's comprehensive plan. The city has pursued VDOT Revenue Sharing Funding as the primary funding mechanism. On May 13, 2025, Council unanimously adopted Resolution R-25-09, endorsing submission of the Dwight Avenue Improvements (along with 1st/2nd Street Sidewalks and Northfax East-West Roadway) to VDOT for Revenue Sharing Funding. On September 9, 2025, Council unanimously adopted an updated Resolution R-25-27, reaffirming submission of the same three projects with an updated application total of up to $10 million.
