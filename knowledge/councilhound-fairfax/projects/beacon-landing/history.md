## 2026-02-26 — HHCAB Committee Meeting (Housing Trust Fund)

**Agenda item 3**: Discussion of Next Steps
- Outcome: The Strategy Team reached consensus on three next steps: (1) focus on the existing $542,524 balance; (2) direct funding toward affordable housing development consistent with the adopted Affordable Housing Strategic Plan and national best practices; and (3) have staff develop policies and procedures for a competitive allocation process, examining Fairfax County's and City of Falls Church's processes. Members also agreed to distribute an AI-generated funding model to support long-term funding identification efforts. No formal vote taken.
- [3] Beacon Landing was identified as a contributing source of gap financing ($700,000 used in February 2023) in the Housing Trust Fund balance timeline, leaving the current net balance at $542,524.
