4010 University Drive (Tax Map 57-4-02-087-B) is a parcel in the CR Commercial Retail zoning district and Old Town Fairfax Historic Overlay District, located at the corner of University Drive and Sager Avenue. The applicant is Pathway Homes, Inc. (agent: Molly Navanti), proposing offices and affordable dwelling units with related support services. The project was flagged in Planning Commission staff reports in November and December 2024 as heading to a City Council public hearing. On December 10, 2024, the City Council approved a Special Use Permit allowing upper-story residential/mixed uses, capped at a maximum of 10 dwelling units, on a 4-3 vote. On February 25, 2025, the City Council unanimously approved a Special Exception to modify the non-residential districts dimensional standards table, allowing a maximum density of 28 dwelling units per acre at the site. Both approvals carry conditions, including a required public access easement along University Drive and Sager Avenue to accommodate future sidewalk widening and undergrounding of utilities, which must be recorded prior to issuance of any zoning or construction permits.
