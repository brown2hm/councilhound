## Open questions

- Zoning permits and construction permits have not yet been issued — recording of the public access easement along University Drive and Sager Avenue is a prerequisite condition that must be fulfilled first.
- The originally described proposal referenced fifteen affordable dwelling units; the approved SUP caps units at 10 while the Special Exception allows up to 28 du/acre — the final unit count and project scope as built remain to be determined through permitting.
