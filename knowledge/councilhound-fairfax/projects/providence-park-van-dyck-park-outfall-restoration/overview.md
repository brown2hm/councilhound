## Official record

The City of Fairfax is restoring stormwater outfalls at Providence Park and Van Dyck Park. Work includes regrading eroded banks, armoring channels with stone, and constructing energy-dissipating pools. Total construction costs are $628,655, partially funded by Stormwater Local Assistance Fund grants.
