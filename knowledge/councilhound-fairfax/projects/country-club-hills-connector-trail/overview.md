The Country Club Hills Connector Trail was a planned trail project in the design phase, associated with at least one grant of approximately $9 million (referenced as roughly $9 million in later public comment). As early as January 2025, the Planning Commission noted waning public interest in the trail. At the January 14, 2025 City Council meeting, Councilmember Bates requested that the trail be included in an upcoming work session discussion alongside the George Snyder Trail and Pickett Road Connector Trail. At the March 11, 2025 work session, Councilmember Bates stated support for canceling the trail and noted more staff time was needed before a formal work session could be held. On April 8, 2025, Councilmember Hall moved to cancel the trail outright, with the council also noting the need to clarify financial obligations related to returning grant funds before proceeding. The matter was scheduled for the April 22, 2025 work session, after which the Council voted 6-0 to terminate all actions with respect to the Country Club Hills Connector Trail. A subsequent July 1, 2025 staff presentation confirmed the trail's cancellation and noted that a stream restoration project in the same area is independent of and unaffected by the trail cancellation.
