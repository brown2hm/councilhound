## Member commentary

- [Councilmember Bates](https://councilhound.net/members/billy-bates) — At the March 11, 2025 work session, Bates stated his support for canceling the Country Club Hills Connector Trail, noting more staff time was needed before a formal work session. At the April 8, 2025 meeting, Bates indicated he expected the matter to wait for a work session before a formal motion was made, expressing some surprise at Hall's immediate motion to cancel.
- [Councilmember Hall](https://councilhound.net/members/stacy-hall) — At the March 11, 2025 work session, Hall requested more information on the connector trails. On April 8, 2025, Hall moved to cancel the Country Club Hills Connector Trail, arguing there appeared to be council support based on prior conversations and noting that Bates himself had raised cancellation as an option even before the current council was elected. Hall also flagged the need to clarify financial obligations — including the implications of returning an approximately $8 million grant — before formally canceling the trail.
- [Councilmember Peterson](https://councilhound.net/members/thomas-peterson) — On April 8, 2025, Peterson seconded Councilmember Hall's motion to cancel the Country Club Hills Connector Trail.
